How the site works

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about recommendations, releases, and reviews.

What is Cozy Corner?

It is a place to discover anime, check upcoming releases, and share favorite anime, shows, and games.

How does Anime Shelf work?

Choose a genre and Anime Shelf uses AniList to select a random group of popular, highly rated matches.

Can I get different suggestions?

Yes. Select Suggest More to add another random group. The page avoids repeating anime that it already showed during your visit.

Are there unlimited recommendations?

You can keep requesting suggestions, but the catalog is not literally infinite. The number available depends on the selected genre and AniList's catalog.

Is the anime appropriate?

The finder removes adult titles and filters out ecchi, horror, nudity, sexual content, and harem tags.

How do upcoming releases update?

Upcoming Releases checks AniList automatically and refreshes its saved results every six hours.

Who can remove a review?

Review controls will be added in a later version of the website.

Are reviews shared with everyone?

Not yet. The current reviews are examples that show how the page will look.
